## 3. Demo Flow — Full Walkthrough (25 min)

### Opening (2 min)
**What to say:**
> "I'm not going to show you slides. Everything I'm about to show you is live — real AI running on real data. I'm going to generate actual leads for YOUR industry, audit YOUR website, and show you the AI receptionist answering real questions. Ready?"

**Why this works:** Immediately differentiates from every other SaaS demo. Prospects lean in when they know it's live.

---

### Act 1: The Hook — Lead Researcher (8 min)

**This is your strongest demo. Always lead with it.**

#### Step 1: Navigate to the Lead Researcher
Go to: `https://products.devcabin.tech/agents/lead-researcher`

**What to say:**
> "Let me show you the product that gets the most 'holy shit' reactions."

#### Step 2: Type Their ICP
In the "Describe your ideal customer" field, type something specific to THEIR business.

**Examples by industry:**
| Client Industry | ICP to Type |
|---|---|
| Marketing agency | `SaaS CMOs at 50-200 person B2B companies using HubSpot` |
| Real estate | `Real estate agents in [their city] with 10+ listings` |
| Restaurant supplier | `Restaurant owners in [their state] with 2-5 locations` |
| SaaS company | `VP of Sales at mid-market SaaS companies with 100-500 employees` |
| Dental practice | `Dental practice owners in [their city] looking for marketing help` |

**What to say:**
> "I'm describing YOUR ideal customer. Watch what happens."

#### Step 3: Click "Find Leads" and Narrate
While it searches (takes 10-15 seconds):
> "Right now the AI is searching real company databases, finding decision-makers, pulling verified emails from Hunter.io, and validating every single email through ZeroBounce — that's the same tool enterprise sales teams pay thousands for."

#### Step 4: Walk Through a Lead Card
Click to expand the first lead. Point out:

1. **The name and title** — "This is a real person. Google them right now if you want."
2. **The score and grade** — "We score every lead on 8 signals. A-grade means verified email, decision-maker title, right company size."
3. **Score breakdown** — "Here's exactly WHY they scored 87 — you can see every signal."
4. **The buying signal** — "This is a real-time reason to reach out NOW."
5. **The blurred email** — "That's a real verified email. It's blurred in the demo but fully visible on paid plans."

#### Step 5: Prove the Leads Are Real (THE CLOSER)
This is the moment that converts skeptics.

**What to say:**
> "I know what you're thinking — 'are these real people?' Let me prove it."

1. **Click the company website link** → Opens their actual website in a new tab
   > "Real company. Real website. You can see their About page right now."

2. **Click "⚡ Verify Domain Can Receive Email"** → Runs live MX lookup
   > "I just ran a live check against this domain's mail servers. See? Google Workspace. This domain receives email. This isn't scraped junk — this is a verified, deliverable inbox."

3. **Click the LinkedIn search link** → Opens LinkedIn with their name + company
   > "There they are on LinkedIn. Real person, real title, real company."

**What to say after:**
> "Every lead goes through 6 layers of validation before you see it. Most lead gen tools skip validation entirely — that's why you get 40% bounce rates. Our emails are ZeroBounce validated. The industry average bounce rate is 12%. Ours is under 3%."

#### Step 6: Show the Data Quality Proof Banner
Point to the green banner below the stats:
> "Look at this — 4 out of 5 leads have verified emails, 3 came from Hunter.io, all 5 have real domains. This isn't AI hallucination — it's real data from real databases."

### Act 2: Website Audit (5 min)

#### Step 1: Navigate to the Auditor
Go to: `https://products.devcabin.tech/agents/website-audit`

**What to say:**
> "While those leads sink in, let me show you something about YOUR website."

#### Step 2: Type Their Domain
Enter the prospect's actual website domain and click "Audit Now."

**While it scans (10-20 seconds), narrate:**
> "This is running a real Google Lighthouse audit right now — the same tool Google uses to decide your search rankings. It's checking performance, SEO, accessibility, and UX."

#### Step 3: Walk Through Results
When results appear:

1. **Score cards** — "Here are your real scores. Performance 62, SEO 78 — let's see what's dragging those down."
2. **Click through issue tabs** — "These are prioritised by traffic impact. This one issue alone could be costing you [X] visits per month."
3. **Check off an issue** — "As you fix things, you check them off. The progress bar fills up."
4. **Traffic gain estimate** — "Based on your current traffic, fixing these issues could bring an extra [X] visits per month."

**What to say:**
> "This is what a $5,000 SEO agency would charge you for. You get unlimited audits for $50/mo. Run it on your competitors too."

#### Step 4: Tech Stack Section (if visible)
> "We also detected your tech stack — WordPress on WP Engine, Google Analytics, Cloudflare CDN. For each one, we show pros, cons, and whether you should upgrade."

## 5. Objection Handling

### "Are the leads actually real?"
> "Let me show you. [Click verify domain button] See that? Google Workspace. That domain accepts email. [Click website link] There's the company website. [Click LinkedIn] There's the person on LinkedIn. We validate every email through ZeroBounce — the same tool enterprise sales teams use. Our bounce rate is under 3%."

### "We already use Apollo / ZoomInfo / Hunter"
> "Great — pull up our compare page. [Show /compare] Apollo charges $99-199/mo for bundled, often stale data. We use live Hunter.io data with ZeroBounce validation on every email. And we show you exactly why each lead scored the way it did — 8 transparent signals. Also — with Scale plan you bring your own API keys and pay zero per-lead markup."

### "We don't have the budget"
> "The Starter plan is $100/mo for 100 verified leads. That's $1/lead. How much does one new customer earn you? Even if only 1 out of 100 converts, you've ROI'd 10-50x."

### "We tried AI tools before and they were junk"
> "This isn't ChatGPT spitting out fake names. Watch — [run demo, verify domain, click website]. Real company, real person, real email, real mail server. Every lead goes through 6 validation layers before you see it."

### "I need to think about it"
> "Totally fair. Can I send you a sample lead list for your exact ICP? No obligation — just so you can see the quality. If you like what you see, I'll help you set up in 5 minutes."

### "What if I don't get results?"
> "Cancel anytime — no contracts, no lock-in. Most clients see ROI in the first week because they're getting verified leads immediately."

### "I don't have time to learn new tools"
> "We offer 1-on-1 AI Training — private Google Meet sessions at $50/hr, or $500 once for unlimited lifetime access with exclusive perks. We'll set everything up together, live, on your screen. Or I can install the receptionist widget on your site for you in under 5 minutes."
