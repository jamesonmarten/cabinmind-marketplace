# Client Guide — CabinMind AI Lead Researcher

For the person actually using the tool. No jargon.

---

## What this does

You describe the kind of business you want to sell to. We find real local
businesses that match, find the right person there, verify their email actually
works, and hand you a list you can email today.

**What makes it different:** anyone can scrape a list of company names. The hard
part is knowing the business is real and the email won't bounce. That's what we
check, and we show you the evidence for every one.

## What you get per lead

| Field | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The actual person, found in a contact database — not a guess |
| **Title** | Their role |
| **Company** | The business name as Google lists it |
| **Domain** | Their real website |
| **Email** | Verified deliverable before you see it |
| **Grade** | A / B / C / D — how good a fit they are |
| **Score** | 0–100, with the reasons shown |
| **Signal** | Why they're worth contacting now |

**You will never see an invented business or a made-up person.** If we can't
find a real contact at a real company, we leave it out rather than fill the gap.
That's why a batch sometimes returns fewer than you asked for.

## Using the trial

Your link looks like `products.devcabin.tech/trial/your-name-here`. No login,
no credit card. It's good for **50 leads, one time** — it does not reset.

### Step 1 — Describe your ideal customer

Be specific about **what** and **where**. Location matters most.

Good:
```
Commercial HVAC contractors in Milwaukee, Wisconsin
CNC machine shops and precision manufacturers in Wisconsin
Independent dental practices in the Chicago suburbs
```

Too vague — you'll get thin results:
```
Small businesses
Companies that need marketing
B2B SaaS
```

There are preset buttons if you want a starting point.

### Step 2 — Choose 5 or 10 per run

Both count against your 50. Ten takes slightly longer.

### Step 3 — Generate

Takes about 15 seconds. Leads appear as cards — click one to expand the full
score breakdown.

### Step 4 — Export

**↓ CSV** downloads everything so far. Opens in Excel, Google Sheets, or imports
straight into your CRM.

## Reading the grades

| Grade | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Senior decision-maker, verified email, strong fit | Email first, personally |
| **B** | Good fit, verified email | Worth a personalised email |
| **C** | Real and verified, weaker seniority or fit match | Fine for a broader sequence |
| **D** | Filtered out before you see it | — |

Expand any lead to see exactly which signals produced the score. Nothing is a
black box.

## Why did I get fewer leads than I asked for?

Because we refused to pad the list. You'll see a message like:

> We checked 63 businesses, ruled out 7 with no working mail domain and 17 with
> no listed contact — rather than invent one.

Usually means the niche is narrow or the area is small. Try:

- **A wider area** — "Wisconsin" instead of "Wauwatosa"
- **A related category** — "mechanical contractors" alongside "HVAC"
- **Plainer words** — describe it how a customer would, not how a marketer would

A short list of real people beats a long list of bounces. One hard bounce hurts
your sending reputation; a few hundred can get your domain blocked.

## Honest limits

- **We find businesses, not consumers.** B2B only.
- **Coverage varies by niche.** Established businesses with real websites are
  well covered. Brand-new or web-less businesses often aren't.
- **We don't send email for you.** You export and send from your own domain, so
  your sender reputation stays yours and compliance stays in your control.
- **Verified doesn't mean interested.** It means the mailbox exists and accepts
  mail. The pitch is still your job.
